Connect a SCIM directory, such as Microsoft Entra ID, to an organization in [Identity Providers](/users/identity-providers). Bifrost creates, updates and deactivates its users and maps directory groups to roles. Organizations verify their email domains with a DNS record first, and directories never grant privileged roles.
